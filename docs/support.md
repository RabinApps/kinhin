# Support & sponsors

kinhin is free, Apache-2.0, and built by one developer, with no ads and no telemetry. Donations and sponsors pay for CI,
code signing, test hardware and development time.

[← Back to README](../README.md)

## Support kinhin

| Where                                                                   | What                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/RabinApps)                | Monthly or one-time, with tiers   |
| [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/donate/?hosted_button_id=YEJ37WF4Q3HPC) | One-time donation                 |
| [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/rabinapps)                   | One-time ($1 to $50+) or $1/month |

`kinhin support` prints the same links, and the menu bar app has **Support kinhin…** in its menu and Help menu.

## Become a sponsor

Companies can sponsor kinhin and have their logo and a link shown on the website's home page and in the app's
**Advanced → App** section. Pick a company tier on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/RabinApps), or email
[support@rabinapps.com](mailto:support@rabinapps.com?subject=kinhin%20sponsorship) with your company name, link and
logo. Sponsorship supports the project; it does not buy influence over the roadmap.
