# Support & sponsors

kinhin is free, Apache-2.0, and built by one developer with no ads and no telemetry. Donations and sponsors pay for CI, code signing and notarization, test hardware and development time.

## Become a sponsor

Companies can sponsor kinhin and have their logo and a link shown on the
website's home page and in the app's **Advanced → App** section.

To become a sponsor, either pick a company tier on
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/RabinApps) or email
[support@rabinapps.com](mailto:support@rabinapps.com?subject=kinhin
sponsorship) with your company name, link and logo. Sponsorship is a way to
support the project; it does not buy influence over the roadmap.

## Companies and projects using kinhin

Small list — shows who else runs kinhin. To add yours, open a PR against
`main` with a single entry following the format in
[external-contributors.md](https://github.com/RabinApps/kinhin/blob/main/docs/external-contributors.md):

```markdown
- [Your Project](https://your-project-url) — one-line blurb of what you use
  kinhin for.
```

No entries yet — your company or project could be the first. A maintainer
reviews the PR for link and wording; once merged, the list on the website and
in the app's **Advanced → App** section is updated.
