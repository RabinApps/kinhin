Support & sponsors

View .mdEdit on GitHub

kinhin is free, Apache-2.0, and built by one developer, with no ads and no telemetry. Donations and sponsors pay for CI, code signing, test hardware and development time.

Become a sponsor#

Companies can sponsor kinhin and have their logo and a link shown on the website's home page and in the app's Advanced → Settings section. Pick a company tier on GitHub Sponsors, or email support@rabinapps.com with your company name, link and logo. Sponsorship supports the project; it does not buy influence over the roadmap.
