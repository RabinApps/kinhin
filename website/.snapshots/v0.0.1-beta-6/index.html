macOS fleet CI · open source · Apache-2.0

Commit it. Screenshot it. Ship it.

Your Mac is your CI, your screenshot studio and your video studio. Ephemeral runners that scale to your queue, store screenshots in every locale, and launch videos that rebuild from your repo. No cloud bill, no watermark.

Get startedWhy kinhin?CLI reference
up and running in three commands
$ curl -fsSL https://github.com/RabinApps/kinhin/releases/latest/download/install.sh | bash
$ kinhin config init && kinhin auth set
$ kinhin image prepare && kinhin start
✓ fleet online

// WHAT YOU GET

What you get

Parallel jobs, not a queue

A fleet of ephemeral runners scales to your queue, so jobs run side by side.

Why kinhin →

Clean every time

Every job gets a fresh VM or container, deleted when the job ends. Nothing leaks between jobs.

Security →

Multi-forge, multi-runtime

GitHub, Gitea, Forgejo and GitLab served from one pool, on Tart, apple/container, Docker or host mode.

Runtimes & routing →

Store screenshots

One fastlane lane captures localized App Store and Play screenshots in CI.

Store screenshots →

Launch videos from a config

Write the story in YAML. Get narration, captions, music and a vertical cut on every release.

Add videos →

Menu bar or headless

The same engine as a macOS app or a LaunchAgent daemon, with one config and one fleet.
